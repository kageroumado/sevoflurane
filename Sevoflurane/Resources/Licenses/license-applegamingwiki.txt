AppleGamingWiki and PCGamingWiki (Mac compatibility data on game pages)
=======================================================================

Compatibility ratings shown on a game's page come from AppleGamingWiki
(https://www.applegamingwiki.com), and the app-id-to-title lookup that finds
the right page comes from PCGamingWiki (https://www.pcgamingwiki.com). Both
wikis publish their content under the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license:

    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/

Each rating in the app links to the wiki page it was read from. The ratings
are shown as the wikis state them; the headline badge is derived from them at
display time and is not redistributed.
