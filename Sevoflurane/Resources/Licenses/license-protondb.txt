ProtonDB (Linux/Proton reports on game pages)
=============================================

Proton tiers shown on a game's page are fetched from ProtonDB
(https://www.protondb.com) for that game alone and cached on your Mac.
ProtonDB's report data is made available under the Open Database License
(ODbL), and rights in its individual contents under the Database Contents
