Simple Icons (the Discord mark on the Steam row)
================================================

https://simpleicons.org · https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

The Discord glyph on the menu bar's Steam row is Simple Icons' tracing of
Discord's logo. Simple Icons is released under CC0 1.0 Universal: the icon
data is dedicated to the public domain. Discord's logo itself remains a
trademark of Discord Inc.; Sevoflurane uses it only to link to its own
Discord server and is not affiliated with Discord.
